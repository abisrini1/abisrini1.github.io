// about

Vision systems that
hold up under scrutiny.

I'm a graduate researcher at Johns Hopkins University, working across segmentation, generative modeling, and image reconstruction — the core problems that show up whenever a model has to turn pixels into structure. My recent work spans 3D volumetric segmentation with nnU-Net and MONAI, GAN-based image synthesis (Pix2Pix, CycleGAN), and denoising with variational autoencoders.

That work happened to be in medical imaging, but the underlying problems — reconstructing signal from noise, tracing structure through cluttered data, evaluating a model beyond a single metric — are the same ones that show up in general computer vision: detection, 3D scene representation, and video understanding. That's the direction I'm building toward next.

I like models I can interrogate. I'd rather spend a week understanding why a segmentation fails than ship a marginal PSNR gain I can't explain.
